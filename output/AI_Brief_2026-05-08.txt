AI DAILY BRIEF — May 08, 2026

OPENING:

Anthropic just pulled back the curtain on how Claude actually thinks — and it's wild. Their interpretability team published research on "Natural Language Autoencoders" that essentially trains Claude to translate its internal numerical representations into human-readable text. This is the closest we've ever gotten to reading an AI's mind, and it could fundamentally change how we understand what these models are doing when they generate responses. If you've ever wondered whether AI "thinks" or just pattern-matches, this research is the first real peek behind that curtain.
