SECTION: Agentic AI and Coding

Mistral launches remote coding agents in Vibe
Mistral's Vibe platform now includes remote agents that can autonomously handle coding tasks — not just assist, but actually work in the background while you do other things. This is powered by their new Mistral Medium 3.5 model, which also brings a new "Work mode" in Le Chat for handling complex, multi-step tasks. This is a direct shot at GitHub Copilot and Cursor, but with a twist: these agents can operate remotely, meaning you can assign work and walk away. For creators, this is a killer demo — show what happens when you give an agent a task and come back an hour later.
Source: https://mistral.ai/news/

Google and Kaggle launch AI Agents Vibe Coding Course
Google is now teaching people how to build with agents through a new course built in partnership with Kaggle. The focus is "vibe coding" — a term that's gaining traction for the intuitive, prompt-driven style of building with AI. This is a strong signal that Google sees agent-based development as the next literacy. Good Short angle: "Google is teaching a new way to code — and you don't need to be a developer to learn it."
Source: https://blog.google/technology/ai/

Anthropic's Project Deal — Claude as a negotiator
Anthropic created an internal marketplace in their San Francisco office where Claude handled all buying, selling, and negotiating on behalf of employees. This is one of the most concrete examples of agentic AI in a real economic environment. The implications are wild — Claude didn't just respond to prompts, it made deals. This is a perfect 60-second explainer: "Anthropic let Claude negotiate for real money. Here's what happened."
